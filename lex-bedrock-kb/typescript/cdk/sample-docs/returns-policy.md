# Returns and refunds

Trail Supply Co. accepts returns of unused items within 45 days of delivery.

To start a return, open the order in your account and choose "Return items". A prepaid
return label is emailed to you within one business day. Return shipping is free for
orders shipped inside the country of purchase.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the
returned items reach our warehouse. Items that show signs of use are refused and sent
back at the customer's expense.

Custom-engraved items and gift cards cannot be returned.
