# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs a flat 6 dollars. Orders of 80
dollars or more ship free with standard shipping.

Express shipping arrives in 1 to 2 business days and costs 18 dollars regardless of
order value.

Orders placed before 2 PM local warehouse time on a business day leave the warehouse
the same day. We do not ship to post office boxes with express shipping.

Every shipment includes a tracking link, which is emailed once the carrier scans the parcel.
