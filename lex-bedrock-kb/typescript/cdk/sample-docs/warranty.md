# Warranty

All Trail Supply Co. tents and backpacks carry a 3-year limited warranty against defects
in materials and workmanship. Sleeping bags carry a 5-year limited warranty.

The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, UV exposure, or normal wear such as
worn zippers on items older than the warranty period.

To make a warranty claim, email the order number and two photos of the defect to the
support team. A decision is sent within 5 business days. Approved claims are repaired or
replaced at our option.
